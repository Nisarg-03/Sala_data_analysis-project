# Sala_data_analysis-project

This document explains the key insights derived from the Sales Data Analysis project and shows
how you can include these insights in your Python data analysis project or portfolio.
Key Business Insights
• Top Selling Product Category: Food and Beverages generated the highest total revenue,
indicating strong customer demand for food-related products.
• Best Performing Branch: Branch A produced the highest total sales compared to other
branches. This may indicate better location advantage or higher customer traffic.
• Most Popular Payment Method: E-wallet was the most frequently used payment method,
suggesting that customers prefer digital payments over cash or credit cards.
